## Evidence treatment

A filled marker means documented, not harmful, authorized, successful or independently verified. Unknown is labelled Not established. Not applicable in R011 refers specifically to live delivery being outside the simulated call. The R020 row examines the supported migration command, not contemplated unauthorized deployment. Running the command does not establish successful session continuity.

Twenty-four disclosures remain uncoded in this comparison; they are not assigned unknown markers. The 29-disclosure collection remains the 18 November snapshot, with authority themes reviewed on 19 November. Only the five matrix sources were re-read on 21 November.

No incident rate, representative sampling, causal estimate, control effectiveness score or new incident classification is introduced.
